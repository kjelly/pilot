- **Fresh node:** the node was recreated, and the whole first-run sequence was replayed in order with the same check after each step. Nothing was left at any point.
- **KDC log:** every `admin@` AS-REQ of the first sequence falls inside one of those playbook runs, all of which use private caches, and the kinit lint finds no default-cache kinit in them.

The source was not found. It is recorded here and not claimed fixed.
