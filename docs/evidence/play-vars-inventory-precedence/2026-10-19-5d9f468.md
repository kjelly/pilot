## Recovery acceptance and actual runs

The common recovery predicate requires active rsyslog plus either a successful rescue restart or two successful, nonempty, identical InvocationID probes. SELinux withdrawal is stricter: the root must have been absent before this run; rescue must have restarted successfully and be active; strict parsing must identify both restored receiver templates outside the new grant's subtree. Pre-existing labels, same/subtree roots, unrecognized formats and uncertain runtime keep their grants. Retained SELinux mappings remain by design on subsequent runs; this is not automatic garbage collection.

Step 7's result was overridden to inject a failure after Step 6a. For a refused rescue restart, a watcher wrote an invalid unmanaged drop-in immediately after InvocationID changed. Existing-label setup removed only the mapping, leaving `var_log_t`, before another failed same-root apply. Each delivery probe opened 24 TCP connections with distinct hostnames and unique nonces, checking newly opened per-host files rather than cached file handles.
