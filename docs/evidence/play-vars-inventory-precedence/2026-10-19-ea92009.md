## Recovery acceptance

Permission withdrawal requires proven runtime recovery. AppArmor needs an active service plus a successful rescue restart or successful, nonempty, identical InvocationID observations. SELinux additionally requires a previously absent root and strictly recognized restored receiver templates outside its subtree. Existing roots/manual labels, same/subtree roots, unknown formats and uncertain runtime retain their grants. Retained mappings are intentionally not garbage-collected by later runs.

The existing-label case removed only a persistent mapping, preserving the live manual label, before a failed same-root apply. Message probes open 24 TCP connections with unique hostnames/nonces and assert received counts on the controller; transport status alone is insufficient.
