## Findings

1. **Identity credential lifecycle limitation (reported, not fixed)** — after revoking then restoring
   Alice's roster membership, `ipa hbactest` correctly grants access again, but a credentialed
   SSH/`kinit` attempt returned `Client's credentials have been revoked`. This is a residual
   limitation of the reconciler's credential-lifecycle handling on a revoke→restore cycle, not
   something this round fixed — flagged for a future round to investigate.
2. The first post-restore identity idempotency rerun reported `changed=1` (password initialization
   ordering relative to user personalization); the final rerun after full personalization correctly
   landed `changed=0`.
3. Two casts are preserved under `casts/failed/` as diagnostics, not evidence: a personalize-users
   run that tripped the secret scanner, and a restored-identity spotcheck that failed — neither
   counted toward the PASS verdicts above.
4. Every accepted cast passed `trec verify`; the recorder reported its own non-blocking "dirty
   build" provenance warning throughout (cosmetic, does not affect verification validity).
