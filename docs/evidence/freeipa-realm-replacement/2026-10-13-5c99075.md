## Regression tests

`TestRegression_RealmReplacementRescueReenrollsTheOldRealm` has a fifth case: the new server's FQDN equals the old one, and the new realm's enrollment is in place. The expected commands are as for a different FQDN:
1. key removal and uninstall;
2. the new enrollment removed;
3. the archived `/etc/hosts` back;
4. the keytab extracted, and the re-enrollment with `--server=ipa1.ipa.pilot.internal --server=ipa2.ipa.pilot.internal`;
5. the archive restored without the keytab.

If the recorded old servers left out the new server's FQDN, the re-enrollment would lose that server; that change makes this case fail. The tests merged from #53 (pin order, the shared-FQDN pin cases, the SSSD snippet) pass here with the extra "record the old enrollment" task.
