## 8. Real bug found and fixed this round

**`delegate_to` on a present-but-empty `seaweedfs-s3` group crashed the
apply.** The Thanos-bucket step used
`delegate_to: "{{ groups.get('seaweedfs-s3', [inventory_hostname]) | first }}"`
guarded by `when: groups.get('seaweedfs-s3', []) | length > 0`. ansible-core
2.19 templates `delegate_to` before evaluating `when`, and `pilot inventory
generate` always emits the group (`hosts: {}`), so the default never applied
and the task failed with `No first item, sequence was empty.` — then the
rescue removed the objstore secret. Earlier evidence runs used vm-target's
own inventory (no such group) and never hit it. Reproduced in isolation
under ansible-core 2.19.2 (old form rc=2, fixed form `skipping`). Fixed in
`prometheus-apply.yml`, `restic-backup-apply.yml`, `thanos-query-apply.yml`
as `(groups.get('seaweedfs-s3', []) + [inventory_hostname]) | first`, locked
by `internal/spec/delegate_to_empty_group_regression_test.go` (commit
`8fafdc6`).
