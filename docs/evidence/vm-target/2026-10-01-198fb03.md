## Not changed here

- In `core-infra-provider.md`, C2, C5 and C6 contain `\|`, which reaches the
  shell as an escaped, literal `|`: C2 runs `ss` with extra arguments (stdout
  `ss utility, iproute2-6.1.0`), C5 asks systemd about units named `|`, `head`
  and `-n1`, and C6 prints the whole `chronyc tracking`. They pass without
  testing what they describe.
- `vm-target rollback`/`reset` and the auto-rollback of `vm-target test` do
  not drop cached facts; a reverted VM keeps the facts gathered after the
  snapshot until the cache times out.
- `pilot deploy` keeps its own fact cache under `<data-dir>/ansible`; a
  vm-target `up`/`down` only cleans the cache its own environment resolves.
- Aliases with `-` trigger Ansible's "Invalid characters were found in group
  names" warning, like every hyphenated group in this repo.
