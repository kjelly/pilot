## Changes in `01a64f4`

- **Which servers run FreeIPA DNS.** The new `tasks/freeipa-dns-providers-live.yml` asks FreeIPA itself: the zone's NS records, queried with `+norec`.
  - A replica that leaves `freeipa_setup_dns` unset and is not listed is left out of the tier's stubs and of the consumer's nameservers. A WARNING names it and the two remedies.
  - An explicit `true` stays in, and fails closed at G5 or at the resolver.
- **The resolver checks every nameserver** before writing anything. It asks FreeIPA servers with `+norec`, and the tier and explicit servers recursively.
- **One forwarder rule.** `tasks/freeipa-dns-forwarders.yml` picks `ipa_dns_forwarders` if it is defined, else `freeipa_dns_forwarders`, else the caller's default.
  - The defaults are `8.8.8.8` on the primary and `[]` on a replica.
  - An explicit empty value means no forwarders on both.
  - Every forwarder must be an IPv4 or IPv6 address, checked before any install.
- **`pilot_diagnose_dns_architecture`** follows the same NS records. An unlisted replica is the finding `freeipa_replica_not_dns_server`.
- **Docs:** the comments, the `freeipa.example.yml` forwarder text, the `freeipa_dns_server_ips` input, the dns runbook gate table, and §0.5.
