## Real captures used by the change

- AlmaLinux 9, NetworkManager 1.54.3: `nmcli -t -f NAME,DEVICE connection show --active` → `System eth0:eth0` / `lo:lo`. `nmcli -g <field> connection show "System eth0"` with DHCP DNS: `ipv4.dns` and `ipv4.dns-search` print an empty line, `ipv4.ignore-auto-dns` prints `no`. With static DNS: `192.0.2.1,192.0.2.2`, `a.example,b.example`, `yes`. Passing those strings back to `nmcli connection modify` restored the same values, and `""` cleared them.
- ansible-core 2.19.2 on the controller: `include_tasks` with `tags: [C1]` and no `apply` runs the include under `--tags C1` but none of the included tasks. With `apply: {tags: [...]}` they run, and a nested include with `apply: {tags: [always]}` runs whenever its include runs.
