  - `5f7c13b` added the matcher. On `6bcfb8a` the rule had none, which is why the S3 drill in `2026-10-13-drills.md` fired.

`0882d2c` makes these changes:

- **Merge with `main` (`f9fb701`).** Only the AGENTS.md changelog conflicted. This PR's row is now v1.52; #79 and #89, still open, add v1.50 and v1.51.
- **HostIOStall.** The rule rates `node_pressure_io_stalled_seconds_total{job="node"}`.
- **promtool fixture.** It uses the exporter's own series shapes:
  - a high stalled (full) series fires;
  - a high waiting (some) series with low stalled stays quiet.
- **Structural test.** It refuses a `kind` label in the rules or the fixture.
- **Fixture Kerberos cache.** `login-probe-fixtures.yml` runs its `kinit` and `ipa` calls in a block with `tasks/freeipa-private-ccache-open.yml`/`-close.yml`, which #72's lint now requires.
