| **BL-1:** the probe-round gate was one scrape interval short. `changes(ts[W])` counts a change only while the sample before it is still in the range, so one seen change holds for W − scrape, not W. The old gate (interval + timeout + 15 s + one scrape ≤ W) accepted {3m, 165s} at a 1m scrape, {3m, 135s} at 30s and {3m, 120s} at 15s.
